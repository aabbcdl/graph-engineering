# Graph Product Quality

Apply the original product review and improvement standards while preserving evidence boundaries.

## Select The Rubric

1. Resolve references relative to this `SKILL.md` file.
2. In `discovery`, `review`, `synthesis`, or `independent_review` work, read [references/review-rubric.md](references/review-rubric.md) completely.
3. In `implementation` or `correction` work, read [references/execution-rubric.md](references/execution-rubric.md) completely.
4. For review-only requests, do not implement improvements.

## Graph Compatibility

- Use the reference as a product judgment rubric, not as the Graph output format. The current node schema, role, user goal, project rules, and safety boundary take precedence.
- Convert each recommendation into the correct mode: direct fix, optimization, experiment, instrumentation, research, or business decision.
- Implement direct, reversible, repository-authorized improvements automatically in write-enabled nodes.
- Do not turn uncertain user demand, conversion impact, market fit, or retention claims into facts. Route them to measurement, an experiment, research, or an owner decision.
- Preserve existing-user value, truthful claims, analytics integrity, monetization fairness, localization, privacy, and cross-surface consistency.

## Decision Authority

- Use repository evidence for low-risk consistency and broken-flow fixes.
- Choose the smallest reversible option for medium-risk product ambiguity and record the assumption.
- Require an owner gate for pricing strategy, material entitlement changes, deceptive or legal claims, irreversible removals, payment behavior, or a business decision not determined by evidence.
- Never release, publish, change live pricing, or mutate production analytics from this skill.

## Verification

Measure the baseline before changing behavior when a meaningful metric or journey is involved. Verify the modified journey, analytics, copy, and existing-user path. Return unresolved hypotheses as explicit follow-up work, not as completed improvements.
